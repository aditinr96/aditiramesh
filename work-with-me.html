I have experience working on a range of research and policy issues at the intersection of technology and society: ad transparency, monetization, anti-monopoly, health data privacy, biometric privacy, AI transparency, and so much more.

I support nonprofits, research institutions, and public interest organizations with research, policy, and campaigns with:

  • Tech policy and digital rights analysis, with a focus on how state surveillance impacts marginalized communities
  • Clear, persuasive writing for policy and public audiences — reports, briefs, op-eds, and messaging
  • Narrative and communications strategy to shift public discourse around Big Tech and mainstream models
  • Qualitative and mixed-methods research to inform storytelling, strategy, and program design
  • Stakeholder and coalition strategy to support advocacy campaigns organizing against Big Tech

Currently, I am most interested in investigating, researching, and mobilizing against state and corporate surveillance, building community-focused privacy tools, and building alternative imaginations of how we use and interact with technology.

If you are an organization in need of research, policy, or campaigns support, find me at aditi-all@proton.me.
