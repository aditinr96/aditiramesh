About Me

I am Tamil-American researcher, writer, policy advocate, and campaign strategist, and have spent much of my career challenging corporate and state control of technology. I am based in Los Angeles, California on Tongva Land.

I have worked with several tech accountability organizations in the US, including Accountable Tech, Check My Ads Institute, and The Governance Lab at NYU. I have researched and written about platform power and antitrust, biometric and gig worker surveillance, carceral technology, and reproductive privacy.

In 2019 and 2020, I helped launch a body of work on data stewardship at the Aapti Institute's Data Economy Lab, to explore data stewardship as a paradigm of governance that re-imagines the data economy.

In 2023, I worked on policy efforts to ban surveillance advertising, and advocate for stronger health data privacy at the state and federal level. I also led a national campaign to petition Google to stop collecting and retaining location data that could reveal the locations of people crossing state lines to seek abortion care.

These days, I am writing and building around the following things:

  • Counter-surveillance and community capacity building — how can we document invisible and visible surveillance infrastructure that shapes urban life through photography and narrative storytelling?
  • Speculative technology — how can we imagine and build a future beyond Big Tech?
  • Digital memory & archiving — how can we use digital tools to preserve memory and physical spaces?

If there's any overlap, I'd love to hear about it!
